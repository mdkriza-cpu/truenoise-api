A fairer balance of noise

Man-made environmental noise is a permanent feature of modern life; we do not promise to end it. Where action is possible, TrueNoise pursues a fairer balance — reducing exposure where we can, and distributing what remains fairly, with extra weight on the most vulnerable. These are proposals to be studied, not demands. Aviation noise around BWI is our first focus area.
The approach

Reduce first, then re-balance

There are two levers, and they are not the same. Altitude reduces — lifting a turn or a route makes every overflight quieter for everyone beneath it. Lateral routing redistributes — it moves noise from one neighborhood to another without lowering the total. Dispersion is not reduction.

So a fair proposal does both, in order: use altitude to lower the total where the physics allow, then distribute whatever remains as fairly as possible — never concentrating a low, loud track over the people least able to bear it.

  • Reduce where we can. Altitude is the one lever that makes every pass quieter, not just someone else's problem.
  • Re-balance the rest honestly. Show every gain and every increase, by neighborhood and by school — no hidden losers.
  • Weight the vulnerable. Schools, and the communities already carrying the most, count for more.
  • Measure, don't assert. Every claim rests on calibrated single-event measurement, not modeled averages.
